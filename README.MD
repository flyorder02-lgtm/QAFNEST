# Design a Premium Minimalist Hospitality Website for QAFNEST

## 1. Project Overview

Design a complete, high-fidelity desktop and mobile-responsive homepage for **QAFNEST — Stays & Hospitality**, a premium hospitality and accommodation brand.

Reference website: https://www.darna-app.com/

Study the reference website's overall simplicity, minimal visual language, clean layout, straightforward navigation, spacing, and ease of use. Use it as design inspiration, but create an original QAFNEST brand experience rather than copying the reference website literally.

The objective is to create a modern, elegant, trustworthy, and conversion-focused hospitality landing page that feels premium without being visually complicated.

**Important: Design only the homepage in this first phase. Do not design additional pages.**

## 2. Brand Identity — Follow Exactly

Use the supplied QAFNEST brand assets and established brand color palette.

Brand name: QAFNEST

Brand descriptor: STAYS & HOSPITALITY

Brand tagline: Feel at home. Stay exceptional.

Brand philosophy: "معايير الضيافة."

### Official Color Palette

- Deep Green — `#0F2D2D`: Primary brand color, navigation accents, buttons, and selected backgrounds.
- Gold — `#D4AF7C`: Premium accent color, subtle highlights, icons, fine borders, and selected decorative details.
- Cream — `#F5EFE6`: Main text color on dark backgrounds, warm neutral surfaces, and subtle background sections.
- Charcoal — `#1A1A1A`: Primary dark text, supporting typography, and neutral contrast.

Use these exact HEX values. Do not replace them with similar colors or introduce unrelated brand colors.

**Color correction:** Use `#0F2D2D` for Deep Green, not `#OF2D2D`, because the letter O is not valid in a hexadecimal color code.

### Brand Asset Requirements

- Use the uploaded QAFNEST logo and emblem as the source of truth.
- Preserve the original logo proportions, shape, typography, and gold treatment.
- Do not redraw, distort, replace, or invent a new logo.
- Display the brand name as QAFNEST and the descriptor as STAYS & HOSPITALITY.
- Use the tagline exactly as supplied: "Feel at home. Stay exceptional."
- Do not add phone numbers, invented contact information, or unsupported awards and certifications.

## 3. Overall Design Direction

Create a refined, minimalist hospitality website inspired by the simplicity and usability of the reference.

The website should feel:

- Premium and sophisticated.
- Warm, welcoming, and peaceful.
- Minimal rather than decorative.
- Architectural and editorial in its visual presentation.
- Easy to understand within the first few seconds.
- Suitable for a premium accommodation and stays brand in Saudi Arabia and the wider hospitality market.

Prioritize whitespace, beautiful typography, a restrained color palette, high-quality hospitality photography, and a clear visual hierarchy.

Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism, neon colors, oversized decorative shapes, complicated animations, unnecessary borders, and crowded content.

The design should feel like a carefully art-directed boutique hospitality brand, not a generic hotel booking template.

## 4. Homepage Structure

### Section A — Minimal Header and Navigation

Create a slim, elegant header with generous horizontal spacing.

Left side:
- QAFNEST logo and emblem.
- Brand descriptor: STAYS & HOSPITALITY, where appropriate.

Right side:
- Home
- Stays
- About
- Contact
- A prominent but understated "Explore Stays" CTA.

Use Deep Green for the primary brand presentation, with appropriate Cream or Charcoal text depending on the background.

Keep the navigation clean and uncluttered. Use subtle hover states and a simple mobile hamburger menu.

Do not create dropdowns or complex navigation systems at this stage.

### Section B — Hero / Above-the-Fold Experience

Make this the strongest section of the entire homepage.

Use a spacious, premium layout with a clear headline, concise supporting text, a primary CTA, and an exceptional hospitality image.

Suggested layout: a split composition with text on one side and a large, beautifully cropped accommodation photograph on the other. An edge-to-edge image with restrained text overlay is also acceptable if it creates a more elegant result.

Suggested content:

Eyebrow:
QAFNEST | STAYS & HOSPITALITY

Main headline:
"Feel at home.
Stay exceptional."

Supporting copy:
"Discover thoughtfully curated stays where comfort, quality, and exceptional hospitality come together."

Primary CTA:
"Explore Stays"

Secondary CTA:
"Discover QAFNEST"

Visual direction:
Use a high-quality photograph of a refined, naturally lit contemporary villa or premium apartment interior. Incorporate warm daylight, natural textures, architectural details, and a calm, welcoming atmosphere.

The photography should feel authentic, sophisticated, and inviting rather than artificial or overly luxurious.

Use Cream and Deep Green as the dominant colors, with small Gold accents.

Keep the headline readable and the CTA immediately visible. Do not overcrowd the hero with multiple buttons, floating widgets, or unnecessary text.

### Section C — Simple Stay Discovery

Below the hero, introduce a clean and intuitive accommodation discovery area.

Use a minimal search interface with only the essential fields:

- Destination or location.
- Check-in date.
- Check-out date.
- Guests.
- Search Stays button.

The search component should feel integrated into the overall design, not like a complicated travel booking engine.

Use subtle borders, clear labels, generous padding, and restrained Gold accents.

If the design tool cannot implement actual search functionality, create a visual prototype only. Do not imply that search or booking is operational.

### Section D — Featured Stays

Create a curated collection of three premium accommodation cards.

Each card should include:
- A large, high-quality property photograph.
- Property name.
- Location.
- Property type, such as villa or apartment.
- A short descriptive line.
- A subtle "View Stay" link.

Use a clean grid with consistent image ratios, elegant typography, and sufficient whitespace.

Avoid heavy card shadows, thick borders, excessive rounded corners, and too much metadata.

Use genuine supplied property imagery if available. If property assets have not been provided, use tasteful visual placeholders or clearly illustrative accommodation imagery. Do not invent actual property listings, prices, ratings, or availability.

Include a minimal "Explore All Stays" CTA below the cards.

### Section E — Brand Introduction

Create a short, editorial-style brand section that communicates the QAFNEST hospitality philosophy.

Suggested heading:
"More than a place to stay."

Suggested supporting copy:
"At QAFNEST, every stay is an opportunity to feel comfortable, connected, and cared for. We believe exceptional hospitality begins with the details that make a place feel like home."

Use an elegant layout with a restrained architectural or lifestyle photograph and generous whitespace.

Include the Arabic brand philosophy, "معايير الضيافة.", only if it fits naturally into the composition.

Do not create a lengthy corporate introduction or a complex brand story section.

### Section F — Why QAFNEST

Present three concise hospitality principles in a simple horizontal layout or three-column grid.

1. Thoughtfully Selected
   "Spaces chosen with comfort, quality, and attention to detail in mind."

2. Comfort That Feels Like Home
   "Welcoming spaces designed to make every stay feel effortless."

3. Hospitality in Every Detail
   "A considered experience focused on care, convenience, and quality."

Use minimal line icons only if they add value. Keep the section visually quiet and avoid oversized illustrations.

### Section G — Closing Call to Action

Create a simple, memorable closing section using Deep Green as the background and Cream typography.

Suggested headline:
"Your next exceptional stay starts here."

Supporting copy:
"Find a space that feels right for you."

Primary CTA:
"Explore Stays"

Use Gold sparingly for the CTA accent or a fine decorative detail.

### Section H — Minimal Footer

Create a clean footer with:

- QAFNEST logo.
- STAYS & HOSPITALITY descriptor.
- A short brand statement.
- Essential navigation links.
- Contact link, if a verified contact destination is supplied.
- Privacy Policy and Terms links as simple visual placeholders for future implementation.
- Copyright line.

Use Deep Green or Charcoal as the footer background with Cream text and subtle Gold details.

Do not invent email addresses, telephone numbers, social media handles, or legal page URLs.

## 5. Typography

Use a premium, contemporary typography system.

Recommended direction:
- Headings: elegant modern serif or a refined editorial display typeface.
- Body: highly legible, clean sans-serif.
- Navigation and buttons: simple sans-serif with restrained font weights.
- Brand descriptor: subtle uppercase lettering with tasteful letter spacing.

Typography should feel sophisticated but remain highly readable.

Avoid excessive font sizes, overly thin text, decorative script fonts, and too many font families.

Use consistent heading levels, line heights, and text widths.

## 6. Layout and Spacing

- Design for a 1440px desktop viewport first.
- Use a centered content container with consistent margins.
- Maintain a clear grid and generous whitespace.
- Keep section heights proportionate and avoid unnecessarily long sections.
- Use consistent spacing throughout the page.
- Align headings, images, cards, and CTAs precisely.
- Keep the first screen focused on the brand, headline, hero image, and primary CTA.
- Make the layout responsive for tablet and mobile screens.
- On mobile, stack content naturally, keep buttons easy to tap, and simplify the search interface without losing its essential fields.

The final homepage should feel compact, intentional, and polished rather than oversized.

## 7. Image Direction

Use premium hospitality photography featuring:

- Contemporary villas and apartments.
- Beautiful architectural interiors.
- Natural daylight.
- Neutral materials and warm textures.
- Refined living rooms and bedrooms.
- Calm, welcoming environments.
- Tasteful Saudi or regional architectural influences where appropriate.

Photography should complement the official QAFNEST color palette rather than compete with it.

Avoid excessive stock-photo collages, unrelated city skylines, generic business imagery, cartoon illustrations, and overly staged luxury scenes.

Use a consistent photographic style across all sections.

## 8. Interaction and Component Design

Create a coherent design system for the homepage.

Include:
- Primary and secondary button styles.
- Navigation links and hover states.
- Property cards.
- Search fields.
- Date and guest selectors.
- Responsive navigation.
- Consistent spacing and typography tokens.
- Accessible contrast and visible focus states.

Keep interactions subtle and purposeful.

Use only restrained transitions, such as gentle image zoom on hover or a small button color transition.

Do not add parallax, large scroll-triggered animations, autoplay video, or elaborate entrance effects.

## 9. Technical and Design Quality

Generate a polished, high-fidelity UI suitable for implementation by a developer after design approval.

Keep components consistent and reusable. Maintain clear alignment, responsive behavior, accessible text contrast, and realistic content density.

The homepage should be visually complete, not merely a wireframe.

Use the official brand assets wherever provided. If any asset is unavailable, use a suitable temporary placeholder rather than fabricating the original brand artwork.

All navigation items and CTA buttons should have clear intended destinations, even if their actual functionality will be implemented later.

## 10. Strict Scope Control

For this iteration, create only the QAFNEST homepage.

Do not design:
- Property detail pages.
- Search results pages.
- Booking and checkout pages.
- Login or registration screens.
- Admin dashboards.
- Separate About or Contact pages.

Do not introduce a new logo, new brand colors, unrelated features, or unnecessary sections.

The final design should preserve the reference website's minimalist spirit while giving QAFNEST its own recognizable hospitality identity.

## Final Objective

Deliver a beautiful, restrained, premium, and implementation-ready QAFNEST homepage with a clear visual hierarchy, elegant hospitality imagery, generous whitespace, intuitive navigation, and the exact official brand colors.

The result should communicate one message immediately:

**QAFNEST — Feel at home. Stay exceptional.**
